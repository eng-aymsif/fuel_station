Setup & Operational Settings: This section forms the operational and financial foundation of the fuel station network, defining invoicing triggers, stock ledger policies, tolerance thresholds, and linking stations to the ERPNext Chart of Accounts.

System Setup Overview#

Before launching pump operations, three foundational documents must be configured:

  1. Register stations and branches in محطة الوقود (Fuel Station) and map them to companies and cost centers.
  2. Define operating rules, shift timings, cash inference, and tolerances in إعدادات تشغيل محطة الوقود (Fuel Operations Settings).
  3. Restrict employee access per station or pump using قاعدة نطاق الصلاحيات التشغيلية (Fuel Operations Scope Rule).

2. Operational Configuration: إعدادات تشغيل محطة الوقود (Fuel Operations Settings)#

The single document إعدادات تشغيل محطة الوقود (Fuel Operations Settings) governs operational behaviors across all branches:

A. Operating Modes & Stock Posting Policies

Field Label (العربية / English)Options & Operational Impact
منشئ الفاتورة (Invoice Creator Mode)Attendant, Supervisor, Cashier, Accountant, Automatic. Defines the role authorized to generate sales invoices.
معتمد الفاتورة (Invoice Submitter Mode)Supervisor, Cashier, Accountant, Automatic (submits invoice immediately upon generation).
توقيت إنشاء الفاتورة (Invoice Timing)Per Dispense (real-time invoice per vehicle), Per Shift (consolidated batch invoice per shift), Per Pump, Manual.
توقيت تسجيل حركة التعبئة (Transaction Capture Timing)Realtime (captured live from meter or attendant UI), Post-Shift (entered at shift close).
توقيت ترحيل المخزون (Stock Posting Timing)On Dispense (instant stock deduction), On Invoice Submit, On Shift Close (bulk stock deduction).

B. Invoice Grouping Configuration

  • تجميع حسب المحطة (Group By Station): Combines events from the same station into one batch invoice.
  • تجميع حسب المضخة (Group By Pump): Issues distinct invoices per dispenser pump.
  • تجميع حسب الوردية (Group By Shift): Groups all sales of the shift together.
  • تجميع حسب عامل المضخة (Group By Attendant): Isolates sales per worker for custody auditing.
  • تجميع حسب الصنف (Group By Item): Separates Gasoline 91, 95, and Diesel invoices.
  • تجميع حسب السعر (Group By Rate): Splits transactions if price revisions occurred mid-shift.
  • تجميع حسب طريقة الدفع (Group By Payment Method): Separates Cash, Card, and Credit sales.
  • تجميع حسب العميل (Group By Customer): Aggregates fleet transactions per corporate client.
  • تجميع حسب مركز التكلفة (Group By Cost Center): Preserves exact departmental cost accounting.
