Issue Orders, Internal Consumption & Cards: When company vehicles, equipment, or generators refuel at company stations, the system avoids generating misleading sales revenue. Instead, it generates ERPNext Stock Entry (Material Issue) documents debited directly to cost centers and expense accounts.

Internal Consumption & Fuel Cards Concept#

Internal fueling and prepaid allowances operate through three core DocTypes:

  1. Issue and approve departmental fuel quotas via أمر صرف الوقود (Fuel Issue Order).
  2. Generate secure time-bound dispensing tokens via تفويض تعبئة الوقود (Fuel Authorization).
  3. Manage RFID smart cards and volume quotas via بطاقة / كوبون وقود (Fuel Card).

1. Issue Order: أمر صرف الوقود (Fuel Issue Order)#

The أمر صرف الوقود (Fuel Issue Order) is an authorization allowing company vehicles or machinery to draw fuel from station tanks:
