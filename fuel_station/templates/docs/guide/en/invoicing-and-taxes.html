Sales, Invoicing, Taxes & POS Settlements: The platform complies with ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing requirements, supporting tax-inclusive fuel rates (15% VAT), flexible real-time or consolidated batch billing, and multi-bank POS card reconciliations.

Invoicing Strategies & Tax Compliance#

Billing operations operate through five interrelated DocTypes:

  1. Aggregate hundreds of dispenser events into a consolidated sales invoice via دفعة فواتير المضخة (Fuel Pump Invoice Batch).
  2. Trace individual dispenses in the child table حدث دفعة فواتير المضخة (Fuel Pump Invoice Batch Event).
  3. Enforce VAT regulations and inclusive pricing in سياسة ضرائب الوقود (Fuel Tax Policy).
  4. Configure bank MDR fee accounts and settlement delays in إعدادات مزود البطاقات (Fuel Card Provider Settings).
  5. Reconcile electronic card POS slips with bank deposits via تسوية البطاقات البنكية (Card Settlement).

1. Batch Invoicing: دفعة فواتير المضخة (Fuel Pump Invoice Batch)#
