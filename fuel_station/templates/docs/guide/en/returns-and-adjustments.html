Returns, Credit Notes & Financial Adjustments: Due to the volatile physical nature of petroleum and contamination hazards once fuel enters a customer's vehicle tank, the system provides strict accounting workflows for operational corrections, Credit Notes, and cash variance settlements without breaching ledger sequence integrity in ERPNext.

Fuel Sales Return Principles#

Fuel returns and corrections are governed by strict internal controls:

  • No Hard Deletions: Posted transactions cannot be deleted. All adjustments must be executed via contra entries and certified credit notes.
  • Decoupling Financial Refunds from Stock Ledger: When billing mistakes are refunded without physical fuel return, financial credit notes are decoupled from stock entries to avoid inventory falsification.

Sales Return & Credit Note Workflow#

  1. Identify the erroneous عملية تعبئة وقود (Fuel Dispense Event) and initiate reversal with mandatory documentation in سبب الإلغاء (Cancellation Reason).
  2. Generate a standard ERPNext Credit Note by clicking Create Credit Note on the associated فاتورة المبيعات (Sales Invoice).
  3. Process the refund based on the original payment channel:
    • Cash Transactions: Record the paid refund in المبالغ المرتجعة (Refunds) inside the attendant's صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box) to reconcile cash custody accurately.
    • Bank POS Card Transactions: Execute POS terminal reversal/void before end-of-day bank settlement.
    • Corporate Credit Fleet: Post the credit note to the client ledger, restoring available credit balance in عقد عميل الوقود (Fuel Customer Contract).
  4. All actions and user identities are permanently logged in سجل تدقيق محطة الوقود (Fuel Audit Log).

Misfueling & Contaminated Fuel Recovery#

If an attendant dispenses the wrong fuel grade into a vehicle (e.g., Petrol 91 into a Diesel engine):

Operational StepStation DocTypeERPNext Ledger Impact
1. Immediately stop engine and siphon contaminated fuel into a dedicated slop drum. عملية معايرة المضخة (Fuel Calibration Event) / حركة المخزون (Stock Entry) Transfer fuel via Material Transfer to waste holding warehouse (never return to main tank).
2. Cancel billing for wrong fuel. فاتورة المبيعات (Sales Invoice Return) Issue full Credit Note canceling customer charges.
3. Dispense correct fuel free of charge as compensation. عملية تعبئة وقود (Fuel Dispense Event) Record dispense charged to station operational error expense account.
4. Write off contaminated mixture. تسوية مخزون الخزان (Fuel Tank Reconciliation) Recognize stock shrinkage under Default Calibration/Waste Expense Account.

Attendant Cash Shortage & Overage Settlements#

When discrepancies occur between counted cash and expected cash in صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box) or خزينة المحطة (Fuel Station Safe):

  • Within Tolerance Threshold: Discrepancies within limits defined in إعدادات تشغيل محطة الوقود (Fuel Operations Settings) post automatically to حساب الفروقات الافتراضي (Default Variance Account) without blocking shift closure.
  • Over Tolerance Discrepancy: Triggers استثناء تسوية الوقود (Fuel Reconciliation Exception) requiring management decision:
    1. Attendant Payroll Deduction: Generate an ERPNext Employee Advance / Deduction entry against attendant salary.
    2. Corporate Expense Write-Off: Approve loss write-off with Operations Manager authorization.
  • Cash Overage Policy: Any surplus cash is credited to Company Miscellaneous Operating Income; operators are prohibited from keeping surplus cash.
