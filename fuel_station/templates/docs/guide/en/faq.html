Frequently Asked Questions & Field Troubleshooting (FAQ): Practical resolutions for real-world scenarios encountered by attendants, supervisors, cashiers, and accountants during daily station operations.

Q1: How do we handle meter rollover when the dispenser counter exceeds max value (e.g. 999,999) and resets to zero?#

Resolution: When totalizers roll over from max capacity to 000000, conventional subtraction produces negative volume. To resolve:

  1. Open the closing قراءة عداد المضخة (Fuel Meter Reading) document.
  2. Check the box هل تم تصفير العداد؟ (Is Meter Reset / Rollover).
  3. The calculation engine automatically applies rollover compensation: Meter Litres = (Meter Max Capacity - Previous Reading) + Current Reading, ensuring accurate volume recognition without distortion.

Q2: How do we manage transactions during a power outage or loss of internet connectivity?#

Resolution:

  1. Dispensers continue pumping using mechanical totalizers or battery-backed electronic boards that never lose cumulative figures.
  2. Attendants record cash and card transactions manually on the official emergency paper register.
  3. When power and internet are restored, supervisors enter closing readings in قراءة عداد المضخة (Fuel Meter Reading) and execute post-shift inference in إعدادات تشغيل محطة الوقود (Fuel Operations Settings) to reconcile cash and electronic collections.

Q3: What happens if a fleet customer exceeds their approved credit limit at the pump?#

Resolution: If license plate entry in عملية تعبئة وقود (Fuel Dispense Event) displays Credit Limit Exceeded:

  1. Dispensing is automatically halted at the pump to eliminate financial risk.
  2. Option A (Immediate Settlement): The corporate customer issues an instant bank transfer. The accountant records an ERPNext Payment Entry against عقد عميل الوقود (Fuel Customer Contract), instantly restoring available balance.
  3. Option B (Authorized Managerial Override): If corporate policy permits, the Operations Manager authorizes an override by recording credentials in معتمد تجاوز الائتمان (Credit Override Approved By) and سبب تجاوز الائتمان (Credit Override Reason), logged in سجل تدقيق محطة الوقود (Fuel Audit Log).

Q4: The bank card POS terminal debited the customer's account, but failed to print a receipt slip. What should be done?#

Resolution:

  1. Print the POS Last Transaction Status from the terminal menu.
  2. If the slip shows Approved with an RRN authorization code, log the transaction in عملية تعبئة وقود (Fuel Dispense Event) with payment method Card and record the RRN in رقم المرجع / الإيصال (Payment Reference).
  3. If cancelled or declined, the customer pays cash or re-swipes. At shift close, compare the terminal settlement report with تسوية البطاقات البنكية (Card Settlement).

Q5: The supervisor forgot to close the prior shift and the next crew has arrived. How do we open the new shift?#

Resolution: The system enforces sequential shift discipline. In emergencies:

  1. The Station Manager creates the new وردية الوقود (Fuel Shift) and enables السماح بفتح استثنائي (Allow Exceptional Open).
  2. Enter the operational justification in سبب الفتح الاستثنائي (Exceptional Open Reason).
  3. Assign the incoming crew to pumps via تكليف عامل بمضخة (Fuel Pump Assignment) immediately.
  4. The outgoing supervisor must immediately complete counting cash drawers in صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box) and close the prior shift.

Q6: A cash shortage was discovered during attendant denomination breakdown. How is it documented and settled?#

Resolution:

  1. Count every banknote and coin systematically in عد فئات النقد (Fuel Denomination Count) inside صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box).
  2. The shortage is auto-calculated in الفرق (عجز / زيادة) (Variance).
  3. If within tolerance, the box closes and posts to حساب الفروقات الافتراضي (Default Variance Account).
  4. If exceeding tolerance, the system generates استثناء تسوية الوقود (Fuel Reconciliation Exception), transfers only actual counted cash to خزينة المحطة (Fuel Station Safe) via تحويل نقدي للخزينة (Cash Transfer), and debits the shortage to employee payroll.

Q7: Official weights & measures inspectors conduct pump calibration during an active shift. How do we record the test volume?#

Resolution:

  1. Record pump meter reading before test discharge.
  2. Inspectors dispense test volume (e.g. 20 Litres) into certified metallic prover cans.
  3. Open عملية معايرة المضخة (Fuel Calibration Event), select pump, enter 20 Litres, and state "Official Calibration Inspection" in السبب (Reason).
  4. Set المعالجة المخزنية (Stock Treatment) to Return to Tank and empty the prover can back into the underground tank opening.
  5. At shift close, تسوية المضخة (Fuel Pump Reconciliation) deducts the 20 Litres from total meter sales, exempting the attendant from cash liability.

Q8: Water detection paste reveals water in a tanker compartment prior to discharge. What is the immediate procedure?#

Resolution:

  1. Halt discharge immediately: Connecting hoses to the underground storage tank is strictly forbidden.
  2. In توريد وقود (Fuel Delivery), set نتيجة فحص عينة الجودة والماء (Sample Quality Result) to Water Detected or Contaminated.
  3. Notify the petroleum supply terminal dispatcher to reject the tanker.
  4. Attach a smartphone photograph of the discolored dipstick paste in Supplier Document Attachment as legal evidence of delivery rejection.

Q9: A cash customer requests a formal ZATCA Tax Invoice with corporate name and VAT number after paying cash. How do we convert the transaction?#

Resolution:

  1. Locate the transaction in عملية تعبئة وقود (Fuel Dispense Event) using the cash receipt number or pump timestamp.
  2. Click Create Sales Invoice.
  3. Change Customer from default retail cash customer to the corporate client registered in ERPNext.
  4. The system validates company VAT registration number and address under سياسة ضرائب الوقود (Fuel Tax Policy).
  5. Submit the invoice to generate the certified standard ZATCA Phase 2 tax invoice with compliant QR code.

Q10: An attendant entered a mistyped meter reading and submitted it. How is it formally corrected?#

Resolution:

  1. Open the faulty قراءة عداد المضخة (Fuel Meter Reading) record.
  2. If meter correction is permitted in إعدادات تشغيل محطة الوقود (Fuel Operations Settings), the supervisor adjusts قيمة القراءة (Reading Value) to the true counter value.
  3. The supervisor records mandatory rationale in سبب التصحيح (Correction Reason) and attaches the meter display photo in صورة العداد (Photo).
  4. The Station Manager approves the correction in معتمد من (Approved By), logging changes permanently in سجل تدقيق محطة الوقود (Fuel Audit Log).

Q11: A shift was accidentally closed before all dispenses were logged. How do we safely reopen it?#

Resolution:

  1. An authorized Station or Operations Manager opens the closed وردية الوقود (Fuel Shift).
  2. Click Reopen Shift.
  3. Input detailed justification in سبب إعادة الفتح (Reopen Reason) and the remediation roadmap in Correction Plan.
  4. Shift status switches to Reopened, unlocking connected صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box) drawers to record delayed dispenses before executing final audited closure.
