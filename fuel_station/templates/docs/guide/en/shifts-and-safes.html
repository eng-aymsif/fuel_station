Strict Financial & Operational Discipline: The system enforces total segregation between an attendant's personal field cash box صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box) and the station's central vault خزينة المحطة (Fuel Station Safe), eliminating co-mingling of funds and pinpointing shortages with pinpoint precision.

Shift & Cash Management Architecture#

Petroleum stations operate around the clock through successive closed shifts. Each shift represents an autonomous accounting cycle governed by eight integrated DocTypes:

  1. Define standard schedules in نوع الوردية (Fuel Shift Type).
  2. Open the operating container in وردية الوقود (Fuel Shift).
  3. Assign attendants and pumps in تكليف عامل بمضخة (Fuel Pump Assignment).
  4. Track individual worker cash drawers in صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box).
  5. Document mid-shift attendant handovers via تسليم واستلام المضخة (Pump Handover).
  6. Count currency notes and coins in عد فئات النقد (Fuel Denomination Count).
  7. Transfer cash collections into خزينة المحطة (Fuel Station Safe) via تحويل نقدي للخزينة (Cash Transfer).
  8. Manage station petty cash and float pools in عهدة النقد (Fuel Cash Custody).

5. Cash Drawer: صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box)#

The صندوق النقد (Fuel Cash Box) calculates expected cash and audits shortages/overages using the canonical formula:

Cash Variance Formula:

Expected Cash = Opening Float + Cash Sales + Approved Receipts + Transfers In - Approved Expenses - Refunds - Transfers Out to Safe

Variance (عجز / زيادة) = Actual Counted Cash - Expected Cash
