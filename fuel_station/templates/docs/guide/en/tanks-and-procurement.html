Tanks, Deliveries, Stock & Calibration: Underground storage tanks function as live physical warehouses. The platform connects bulk road tanker delivery documents with physical dipstick charts and automatic tank gauging (ATG) probes, generating standard ERPNext Stock and Purchase receipts.

Tanks & Fuel Procurement Overview#

Fuel inventory management is executed through four core DocTypes:

  1. Map physical tanks to ERPNext warehouses in خزان الوقود (Fuel Tank).
  2. Document road tanker discharges, ullage, and water paste tests in توريد وقود (Fuel Delivery).
  3. Deduct calibration prover volume from retail meter sales in عملية معايرة المضخة (Fuel Calibration Event).
  4. Reconcile book stock against dip readings via تسوية مخزون الخزان (Fuel Tank Reconciliation).
