Users, Roles & Permissions: The system enforces enterprise-grade governance and strict Segregation of Duties (SOD). No single operator can initiate, execute, approve, and reconcile fuel transactions without supervisory oversight.

The Seven Standard Station Roles#

Operations are structured around seven standard station roles:

#Station Role (العربية / English)Authorized Permissions & DutiesRestrictions & SOD Safeguards
1 عامل المضخة (Fuel Attendant) Capture meter readings, record dispense events, count cash custody in personal cash box, execute pump handovers. Strictly prohibited from altering historical readings, voiding invoices, opening/closing shifts, or modifying selling rates.
2 مشرف الوردية (Shift Supervisor) Open/close shifts, assign attendants to pumps, receive cash transfers to safe, approve minor variances within tolerance. Prohibited from posting final General Ledger entries or modifying company Chart of Accounts and tax templates.
3 أمين الصندوق / الكاشير (Cashier) Receive cash from attendants, deposit cash into station safe, manage opening floats, reconcile POS electronic slips. Prohibited from modifying dispenser meter counters or generating internal consumption issue orders.
4 محاسب المحطة (Accountant) Review and post sales invoices, create GL journal entries, perform bank card POS settlements, reconcile stock ledgers. Prohibited from executing field dispensing operations or performing physical pump handovers.
5 مسؤول المخزون (Inventory Officer) Receive tanker fuel deliveries, take manual dipstick and ATG measurements, document calibration events, reconcile tank stock. Prohibited from billing retail customers or collecting cash collections.
6 مدير المحطة (Station Manager) Overall branch supervision, approve large discrepancy exceptions, authorize exceptional shift reopenings in emergency. All executive overrides are permanently recorded in system audit logs.
7 مدير العمليات (Fuel Operations Manager) Configure global system parameters, manage company-wide fuel pricing, approve corporate credit limits across all stations. Full administrative and operational authority.
