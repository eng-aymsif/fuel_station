Reconciliation & Reports: Reconciliation acts as the financial safeguard of the station. The engine reconciles meter counters against cash, bank POS cards, fleet credit accounts, and internal issue orders, escalating over-tolerance discrepancies through structured approval matrices.

Reconciliation & Dispute Resolution Cycle#

Shift settlement and auditing operate through two primary records alongside operational reporting registers:

  1. Perform pump reconciliation in تسوية المضخة (Fuel Pump Reconciliation).
  2. Escalate over-tolerance discrepancies into استثناء تسوية الوقود (Fuel Reconciliation Exception).
  3. Extract live registers and discrepancy summaries to ensure financial closure.

Standard Operational Reports#

  • Daily Fuel Sales Register: Comprehensive breakdown of fuel dispenses by nozzle, payment mode, and VAT.
  • Pump Meter Reconciliation Report: Meter counter delta versus logged sales volume per dispenser.
  • Cash Box Variances Report: Shift-by-shift attendant cash shortages and overages with denomination details.
  • Tank Stock Movement & Dip Balance: Comparison of live ATG/dipstick measurements against ERPNext Stock Ledger.
  • Fleet Customer Consumption Report: Real-time corporate client consumption and remaining credit limits.
