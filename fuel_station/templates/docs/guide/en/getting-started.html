Comprehensive Operational User Manual: Designed for all fuel station staff (Pump Attendants, Shift Supervisors, Cashiers, Accountants, Inventory Officers, and Operations Managers). Built with absolute accounting and stock integrity on ERPNext with zero parallel ledgers.

What is Fuel Station Management System?#

The Fuel Station application is an enterprise solution for petroleum stations and fuel retailers (Gasoline 91, Gasoline 95, Diesel, Gas, Kerosene). It directly connects physical mechanical and electronic pump meter counters to underground storage tanks, converting every dispensed drop into real-time accounting journal entries and stock movements in ERPNext.

The Core Principle (Zero Parallel Ledgers): No separate hidden ledgers or auxiliary books exist outside the company's Chart of Accounts and Warehouses tree. Every single dispense event generates direct effects on the General Ledger and Stock Ledger in accordance with GAAP, IFRS, and ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing standards.

DocType and Field Nomenclature Standards#

To provide maximum clarity across multilingual operational teams:

  • All DocType names are formatted in bold with both Arabic and English names: e.g. وردية الوقود (Fuel Shift).
  • All field labels are consistently displayed with both Arabic and English labels: e.g. العهدة النقدية الافتتاحية (Opening Cash Float).
