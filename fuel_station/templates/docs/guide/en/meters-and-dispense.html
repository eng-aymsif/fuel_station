Meters & Dispense Operations: Dispenser meters represent the physical reality of petroleum movements. The system reconciles physical meter counter deltas against operational sales events, ensuring zero unbilled fuel loss.

Operational Workflow of Fuel Dispensing#

Fuel dispensing relies on three foundational DocTypes:

  1. Configure nozzles, connected tanks, and pulse meters in مضخة الوقود (Fuel Pump).
  2. Record verified opening and closing meter counters in قراءة عداد المضخة (Fuel Meter Reading).
  3. Log every single vehicle or customer fill transaction in عملية تعبئة وقود (Fuel Dispense Event).

Cash Inference Formula#

When high station throughput prevents attendants from manually recording every cash customer in real time, the system computes inferred cash sales using meter residuals:

Meter Residual Inference:

Inferred Cash Liters = Meter Reading Delta - (Card Liters + Fleet Liters + Internal Issue Liters + Calibration Liters)

Expected Cash Amount = Inferred Cash Liters × Tax-Inclusive Rate
