Customers, Fleets & Credit Limits: The platform delivers an advanced risk management ecosystem for corporate and credit fleets, combining real-time true credit exposure calculations to eliminate default risk with fine-grained vehicle and fuel restrictions.

Corporate Fleet & Credit Operations Overview#

Fleet services rely on three core records:

  1. Register fleet vehicles, approved fuel items, and tank volumes in مركبة العميل (Customer Vehicle).
  2. Document authorized drivers and contact credentials in سائق العميل (Customer Driver).
  3. Define legal credit limits, pricing, and billing periods in عقد عميل الوقود (Fuel Customer Contract).

True Credit Exposure Health Check#

Unlike conventional systems that only inspect ledger aging reports, the engine dynamically checks live operational exposure prior to releasing nozzle fuel:

Live Credit Health Formula:

Available Credit Balance = Approved Credit Limit - [Outstanding Posted Sales Invoices + Unbilled Real-time Dispense Events - Unallocated Customer Receipts]

Automatic Policy: If Current Dispense Estimate > Available Credit ➔ Dispenser immediately halts authorization.
